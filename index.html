Introduction to the Game

Metal Gear Solid is a critically acclaimed stealth action video game developed by Konami and released for the PlayStation in 1998. Directed by Hideo Kojima, the game follows Solid Snake, a retired soldier who infiltrates a nuclear weapons disposal facility to neutralize a terrorist threat from Foxhound, a renegade special forces unit.
