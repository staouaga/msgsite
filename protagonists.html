Key Characters & Allies

The heroes of Metal Gear Solid risk everything to prevent a nuclear catastrophe and expose government cover-ups.

Key Personnel Directory

Solid Snake
The legendary infiltration expert and former Foxhound operative tasked with stopping the terrorist uprising.
Roy Campbell
The commanding officer guiding Snake via codec support, bringing vital tactical intelligence.
Meryl Silverburgh
Campbell's rookie niece caught in the facility takeover who fights alongside Snake.
Dr. Hal Emmerich (Otacon)
The chief engineer of the Metal Gear REX project who assists Snake with high-tech support.

Core Allies Overview

  • Specialists: Experts in intelligence, hacking, and weapon systems.
  • Field Operatives: Trained agents operating behind enemy lines.
